Welcome to MacTiles __VERSION__

MacTiles brings the Windows 8 Start screen to your Mac: colorful tiles, named groups and Live Tiles that show the time, weather, your next meeting, headlines and more.

  • MacTiles is installed in your Applications folder.
  • It opens when you log in, and Start stays on your desktop behind your apps.
  • If an older copy of MacTiles is running, it is closed and replaced. Your tiles and settings are kept.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
